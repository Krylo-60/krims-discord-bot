Server Policy

Official Guidelines

Live synced from Discord & Database. Please respect our guidelines to keep KryloSMP fun and competitive for everyone.

1. Respect & Fair Play
Treat all players, staff, and creators with mutual respect. Zero tolerance for harassment, racism, homophobia, toxicity, or hate speech.
⚠️ Punishment: Warning ➔ 24h Mute ➔ Permanent Ban
2. No Spam or Self-Promotion
Advertising other servers, unsolicited DMs, repetitive chat spam, and referral links are strictly prohibited.
⚠️ Punishment: 12h Mute ➔ Permanent Mute
3. Account & Security
You are fully responsible for your account. Compromised accounts, ban evasion with alt accounts, and sharing logins are not permitted.
⚠️ Punishment: Blacklist & IP Ban
4. No Hacking or Unfair Advantages
X-Ray texture packs, Killaura, Fly, Speed, Auto-clickers (>15 CPS), Baritone bots, and unapproved mods are strictly banned.
⚠️ Punishment: Instant 30-Day Ban ➔ Permanent Blacklist
5. No Bug Abuse or Duplication
Exploiting game glitches, item duping, crash exploits, or glitching through world borders is strictly forbidden. Disclose bugs in #support for massive rewards.
⚠️ Punishment: Inventory Wipe + Permanent Ban
6. Combat & Combat-Logging
Disconnecting, teleporting, or quitting while in active PvP combat triggers automatic death and heart loss via our combat tag engine.
⚠️ Punishment: Automatic Combat-Death & Heart Loss
7. Trap & Spawn Etiquette
Spawn trapping portals, killing players immediately at neutral hub warps, or building lag machines is prohibited.
⚠️ Punishment: Structure Removal + 3-Day Ban
8. Real Money Trading (RMT) Policy
Trading in-game items, hearts, or KryloCoins (KC) for real-world currency outside official web links is strictly prohibited.
⚠️ Punishment: Permanent Account Ban
9. Fair Trading & Scamming
Scamming players during agreed item trades or auction transactions is punishable.
⚠️ Punishment: Trade Reversal + 7-Day Ban
