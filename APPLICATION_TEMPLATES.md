# 📋 KryloSMP Official Application Templates & Google Form Blueprints

Use these templates to quickly set up your Google Forms on [forms.google.com](https://forms.google.com/), or use the automated Discord `/apply` modal built into KryloSMP!

---

## 1. 🛡️ Staff Application (Moderator / Admin / Helper)

**Form Title:** `KryloSMP — Staff Application Form`  
**Description:** `Thank you for your interest in joining the KryloSMP Staff Team! Please answer all questions honestly. Low-effort or troll applications will be immediately denied.`

### Section 1: Basic Information
1. **Discord Username & User ID** *(Short answer - Required)*  
   *Example: `krylo_official (1524878881918685405)`*
2. **Minecraft In-Game Name (IGN)** *(Short answer - Required)*  
   *Are you Java, Bedrock, or both?*
3. **Age** *(Short answer - Required)*  
   *Must be 13+ to comply with Discord TOS.*
4. **Timezone / Region** *(Dropdown/Short answer - Required)*  
   *e.g. EST (UTC-5), GMT (UTC+0), IST (UTC+5:30), etc.*
5. **Position Applying For:** *(Multiple choice - Required)*
   - 🛡️ Moderator
   - ⚡ Admin
   - 🤝 Community Helper
   - 🏗️ Builder / Developer

---

### Section 2: Experience & Availability
6. **How long have you been playing on KryloSMP?** *(Short answer - Required)*
7. **How many hours per week can you dedicate to KryloSMP?** *(Multiple choice - Required)*
   - 5 - 10 hours
   - 10 - 20 hours
   - 20+ hours
8. **Do you have previous staff or moderation experience on Minecraft / Discord servers?** *(Paragraph - Required)*  
   *If yes, list server names, player counts, your role, and why you left.*
9. **What tools / commands are you familiar with?** *(Checkboxes)*
   - CoreProtect (`/co i`, `/co rollback`)
   - Discord Moderation (Timeout, Kick, Ban, Audit Logs)
   - EssentialsX (`/mute`, `/warn`, `/jail`, `/tempban`)
   - AutoMod / Ticket Systems

---

### Section 3: Scenario & Judgment Questions
10. **Scenario 1:** A player is accused of using X-ray/KillAura, but there is no staff online with video evidence. How do you handle the situation? *(Paragraph - Required)*
11. **Scenario 2:** Two players are arguing aggressively in `#general-chat` and using toxic language. What steps do you take? *(Paragraph - Required)*
12. **Scenario 3:** A close friend of yours violates server rules (e.g. griefing or spamming). How do you treat them? *(Paragraph - Required)*
13. **Why should we choose YOU over other applicants?** *(Paragraph - Required)*

## 2. 🤝 Server Partnership Application

**Form Title:** `KryloSMP — Partnership Application Form`  
**Description:** `We partner with active Discord servers, Minecraft networks, and gaming communities to grow together!`

### Requirements:
- Minimum **50+ active members** (excluding bots)
- Family-friendly and adheres to Discord Terms of Service
- Must have a dedicated `#partnerships` channel

### Questions:
1. **Your Discord Username & Server Owner Status** *(Short answer - Required)*
2. **Server / Community Name** *(Short answer - Required)*
3. **Permanent Discord Server Invite Link** *(Short answer - Required)*
4. **Current Total Member Count (excluding bots)** *(Short answer - Required)*
5. **What is your server's primary theme / focus?** *(Multiple choice - Required)*
   - Minecraft SMP / Network
   - Gaming Community
   - Content Creator / Fan Club
   - Anime / Social Hub
   - Other
6. **Partnership Type Requested:** *(Multiple choice - Required)*
   - Standard Cross-Promotion (Ad Swap)
   - Event Collaboration (Joint Tournaments)
   - Content Creator Spotlight
7. **Your Server's Promotional Advertisement Text** *(Paragraph - Required)*
